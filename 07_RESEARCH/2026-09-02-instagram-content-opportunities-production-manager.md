## Evidence Record 1 — B2B Instagram Reels: process-breakdown format shows lead-gen signal

**Evidence Type:** INDUSTRY SOURCE (Tier 4 — reliable independent marketing-industry source, not first-party, not Malaysia-specific)
**Source:** Markana Media, "B2B Instagram Reels: Ideas That Drive Pipeline Growth," accessed 2026-09-02
**URL:** https://markanamedia.com/blog/b2b-instagram-reels-ideas/
**Publication date:** dated 2026 (article states "July 10, 2026" per search metadata)
**Geography:** Not Malaysia-specific — general B2B (US-market-oriented agency)
**Relevant Audience:** B2B buyers generally, not Production-Manager-specific
**Relevant Platform:** Instagram Reels
**Relevant Product:** Not Mysoft-specific

**Statement/Observation:** The source states that a 90-second "process breakdown" Reel format — showing a manufacturing client's quality-control methodology step by step — reportedly generated 47 qualified leads in Q1 2026. The source also states Instagram's 2026 algorithm favors watch-through rate and saves over raw posting frequency, and recommends 3–5 Reels/week as a sustainable cadence.

**Interpretation:** This is a single vendor-reported case study (one client, one quarter), not an independently verified, peer-reviewed, or first-party result. Per `00_SYSTEM/evidence_rules.md` §19 (sample size), one reported case is an OBSERVATION, not a validated pattern — it should not be treated as proof this format will work for Mysoft MES.

**Supporting evidence:** A second Tier 4 source (c3digitus.com, "What Social Media Platforms Should a Manufacturing Company Use in 2026?", accessed 2026-09-02, https://c3digitus.com/blog/social-media-platforms-manufacturing-companies-2026/) independently states that a single production-floor content shoot can generate 15–20 Instagram Reels/TikTok clips, and separately argues manufacturers should prioritize "consistently sharing expertise, demonstrating capabilities" over trend-chasing. This is a different agency/source making a directionally similar point (process/expertise-led content over lifestyle content), which per `evidence_rules.md` §21 counts as weak independent corroboration — not strong, since both are marketing-agency blog content rather than first-party data.

**Contradicting evidence:** This finding sits in some tension with the already-filed `03_PLATFORM/instagram.md` (2026-08-31) conclusion that Instagram is a weaker primary channel for MES lead-gen in Malaysia specifically (younger/lifestyle-skewed audience, MES buyers more Facebook/LinkedIn-weighted) and should be treated as secondary/employer-branding. The new evidence here is about Instagram Reels format performance in a general/US B2B context, not about whether the *Malaysian* Production-Manager audience is present on Instagram at all. Both conclusions can be true simultaneously: IF Mysoft posts on Instagram (for the reasons already on file — under-invested channel, low competition, useful for reaching younger digital-transformation-adjacent staff), a process-breakdown format may outperform other formats available to it — but this does not override the existing recommendation that Instagram stay secondary to Facebook/LinkedIn for Malaysian MES lead-gen.

**Confidence:** LOW-MEDIUM. Not Malaysia-specific, not MES-specific, single-case-study sourced, Tier 4 (marketing-agency blog, not primary/first-party).

**Evidence gaps:** No Malaysia-specific or MES-category-specific confirmation that process-breakdown Reels perform this way. No Mysoft first-party Instagram performance data exists yet (`06_PERFORMANCE/video_performance.md` — TO VERIFY, not checked as populated for Instagram in this pass beyond confirming no contradicting internal data was found in files read).
