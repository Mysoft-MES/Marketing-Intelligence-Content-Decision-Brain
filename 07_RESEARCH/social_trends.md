## 2026-09-02 — Cross-platform B2B content trends

> Builds on `2026-08-31-malaysia-social-media-trends.md` (Facebook/Instagram/Reddit
> for the Malaysian SME MES segment). This pass adds the **LinkedIn B2B / thought-
> leadership** dimension flagged as a gap in that doc. All figures below are
> **external benchmarks → TESTING, never VALIDATED** per `evidence_rules.md`; none is
> Mysoft first-party data. Access date: 2026-09-02.

### S1 — LinkedIn thought-leadership is the under-weighted B2B channel

- **Platform:** LinkedIn.
- **Trend:** 2026 B2B marketing coverage consistently reports thought-leadership content
  (original articles, data/research, video interviews) materially outperforming
  promotional/job content on engagement and shareability, and a large share of B2B
  marketers planning to increase original-research and video content in 2026.
  Manufacturing-specific advice: lead with operational-efficiency / supply-chain /
  maintenance insight rather than product talk.
- **Audience relevance:** HIGH — LinkedIn is where Mysoft's buying committee
  (plant/ops/production managers, GMs, IT, digital transformation) is reachable in a
  professional context; the 2026-08-31 doc already flagged LinkedIn as likely
  under-indexed.
- **Evidence and sample size:** Multiple 2026 industry write-ups (MarketingProfs / eMarketer
  data cut, RevNew, others). Aggregated marketer surveys, not category- or
  Malaysia-specific; directional only.
- **Possible implication:** Prioritise a LinkedIn thought-leadership track for Mysoft:
  operational-insight posts and short interview clips aimed at production/plant managers,
  problem-first not product-first. This is consistent with the Brain's existing
  problem-led narrative. The September LinkedIn calendar (already awaiting approval) is
  the vehicle to test it.
- **Recommended status:** TEST.
- **Confidence:** LOW-MEDIUM (external benchmark).
- **Last checked:** 2026-09-02.

### S2 — Short-form vertical video is the default, including B2B/industrial

- **Platform:** Cross-platform (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn).
- **Trend:** Continued 2026 signal that short-form vertical video is the baseline
  content format and the main organic-reach driver; static image posts and
  hashtag-led strategies keep declining. B2B video marketers report short-form social
  video as their highest-ROI video format.
- **Audience relevance:** MEDIUM-HIGH — reach mechanism applies even for a niche
  industrial audience; the first 1–2 seconds carry the hook.
- **Evidence and sample size:** 2026 platform/marketing commentary; consistent with the
  2026-08-31 Malaysia pass. Not Mysoft-measured.
- **Possible implication:** Keep production effort centred on short, hook-first vertical
  video (factory scenario, tablet-replaces-paper, dashboard-in-use). Reuse across
  FB/IG/LinkedIn with per-platform hook and caption adaptation, not identical reposting
  (`decision_framework.md` §15).
- **Recommended status:** MONITOR (already the working assumption) / TEST at execution.
- **Confidence:** LOW-MEDIUM (external benchmark).
- **Last checked:** 2026-09-02.

### S3 — "Authentic over polished" continues to outperform

- **Platform:** Cross-platform.
- **Trend:** Raw, specific, honest "here's a real problem and how it actually looks"
  content continues to beat glossy corporate video in 2026 commentary — reinforced by
  Reddit-tone findings in the 2026-08-31 doc (audiences reward concrete detail, punish
  vendor-speak).
- **Audience relevance:** HIGH — fits Mysoft's "invisible loss / production truth"
  narrative and a skeptical operations audience.
- **Evidence and sample size:** Broad 2026 social commentary; not Malaysia- or
  category-specific, not Mysoft-measured.
- **Possible implication:** Favour real shop-floor footage, real screens, plain
  language and a single clear point per clip over branded animation. Feeds
  `05_CREATIVE/creative_rules.md` once tested.
- **Recommended status:** TEST.
- **Confidence:** LOW-MEDIUM (external benchmark).
- **Last checked:** 2026-09-02.

### Carried forward from 2026-08-31 (still current, not re-evidenced this pass)

- Facebook = highest-reach, cost-efficient discovery channel in Malaysia; 35+ skew fits
  the buying committee; journeys funnel into WhatsApp to close.
- Instagram = secondary / employer-branding, demographic mismatch with core buyers.
- Reddit = no meaningful local Malaysian SME MES discussion; participate, don't promote,
  if used at all.
