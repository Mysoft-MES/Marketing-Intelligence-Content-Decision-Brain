# 3. WHAT BUSINESS ARE WE IN?

Mysoft MES operates in the manufacturing digitalisation and production management technology market.

The business helps manufacturers move from production environments that may depend heavily on:

- paper
- manual records
- spreadsheets
- disconnected information
- verbal updates
- manually prepared reports
- experience-based management

toward more structured, visible and traceable production information.

The Brain should understand that Mysoft MES is not simply selling software features.

The broader customer problem is:

HOW CAN A MANUFACTURER BETTER SEE, TRACK, VERIFY AND MANAGE WHAT IS ACTUALLY HAPPENING ON THE SHOP FLOOR?

---

# 4. CORE BUSINESS PROBLEMS

The central problems Mysoft MES addresses include:

LACK OF PRODUCTION VISIBILITY

+

UNRELIABLE PRODUCTION REPORTING

+

HUMAN ERROR IN MANUAL RECORDING

+

FALSE OR INACCURATE PRODUCTION CLAIMS

+

POOR TRACEABILITY

+

DELAYED INFORMATION.

These problems may appear in different forms:

- management cannot easily see actual production status
- production managers need to manually check where a job is
- supervisors depend on operators or other staff to report production progress
- production information is recorded manually
- paper Job Travellers may contain incomplete or incorrect information
- production quantities may be entered incorrectly
- operators may forget to record production information
- information may be recorded late
- information may be duplicated
- information may be missed
- information may be recorded against the wrong job
- reported output may differ from actual output
- reported working time may differ from actual production activity
- reported job progress may not reflect what is actually happening on the shop floor
- management may receive inaccurate production reports because of human error
- management may receive false or incorrect production claims that are difficult to verify
- production information may pass through several people before reaching management
- production problems may only become visible after reports are submitted
- responsibility for mistakes or discrepancies may be difficult to identify
- management may have difficulty determining who performed a particular activity
- management may have difficulty determining when an activity actually occurred
- production information may be fragmented across paper, Excel, whiteboards, ERP and other systems
- actual production information may be delayed
- management may make decisions based on incomplete, delayed or inaccurate information

The underlying business problem is therefore not simply:

"WE CANNOT SEE PRODUCTION."

It can also be:

"WE CANNOT BE CERTAIN THAT THE PRODUCTION INFORMATION WE RECEIVE ACCURATELY REFLECTS WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED."

This creates a need for better:

VISIBILITY

+

DATA CAPTURE

+

ACCOUNTABILITY

+

TRACEABILITY

+

VERIFICATION

+

INFORMATION RELIABILITY.

Mysoft MES should therefore be understood not only as a system for viewing production information.

It can help create a more structured and traceable digital record of shop-floor activity.

This may help management answer:

- What is happening now?
- Where is the job?
- Who is working on it?
- When did the activity occur?
- When was it recorded?
- How much was produced?
- What happened at each production stage?
- What was reported?
- Can the reported information be verified?
- Where did a discrepancy occur?
- Who or which process recorded the information?
- Can the production history be traced?
- Are we making decisions based on actual production information or someone's report of what happened?

These problems must still be validated by audience, industry and actual customer evidence before the Brain assumes every manufacturer experiences them equally.
