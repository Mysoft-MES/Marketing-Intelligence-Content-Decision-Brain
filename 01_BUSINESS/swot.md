# 3. SWOT CLASSIFICATION

The Brain should use the following distinction.

## STRENGTH

An INTERNAL characteristic that may help Mysoft MES compete, deliver value or create an advantage.

## WEAKNESS

An INTERNAL limitation that may reduce Mysoft MES's ability to compete, sell, implement, scale or deliver value.

## OPPORTUNITY

An EXTERNAL market condition, customer need, technology change, competitor weakness or emerging demand that Mysoft MES may be able to exploit.

## THREAT

An EXTERNAL condition that may reduce demand, increase competition, weaken differentiation or create business risk.

Important:

INTERNAL = STRENGTH / WEAKNESS

EXTERNAL = OPPORTUNITY / THREAT.

Do not place the same issue in multiple categories without explaining why.
