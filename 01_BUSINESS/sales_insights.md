## 2. WHAT TO CAPTURE FROM EACH DEMO (going forward)

When a demo happens, add a dated entry with whatever of the following is real:

- **Date / prospect industry / company size / roles in the room**
- **How the lead came in** (referral, event, inbound, outbound, workshop contact…)
- **What problem the prospect described in their own words** → also copy the raw quote to
  `07_RESEARCH/customer_insights.md`
- **Which parts of the demo they reacted to** (Digital Job Order, shop-floor visibility,
  traceability, the paper-vs-digital contrast…)
- **Objections / hesitations raised** (price, "too small for MES", data-entry burden, ERP
  fit, adoption, "we'll build it ourselves", timing/budget…)
- **What they compared Mysoft to** (a named competitor, Excel/paper, their ERP, doing
  nothing)
- **Next step and outcome** (proposal, pilot, stalled, lost — and why)
- **Any capability they asked for that Mysoft does not have** → route to
  `01_BUSINESS/products.md` §39 product-opportunity flow (via proposal)
