- **Role and responsibilities:** Production status, material requirements, schedule changes, WIP status, delivery impact, reliability of production updates feeding planning. VERIFIED FACT (internal business knowledge, `01_BUSINESS/company_profile.md` §14), HIGH confidence.
- **KPIs:** Schedule adherence, inventory turns, on-time delivery, plan-vs-actual variance. INDUSTRY SOURCE — Method, "Production planning and scheduling: A 2026 guide for manufacturers," accessed 2026-09-02; Onepint, "What Is Production Planning?," accessed 2026-09-02. LOW-MEDIUM confidence (international, not Malaysia-specific).
- **Daily frustrations:** Only 16% of manufacturing leaders report real-time work-in-process (WIP) monitoring throughout the full production process; without it, planning relies on stale or incomplete data, producing bottlenecks and unreliable downstream decisions. INDUSTRY SOURCE — NetSuite, "5 Top Challenges in Production Planning," accessed 2026-09-02. LOW-MEDIUM confidence (statistic's underlying methodology not disclosed).
- **Operational problems:** The IT/OT convergence gap — the disconnect between enterprise systems (ERP) and shop-floor systems (MES, machine sensors) — is identified as a core driver of this visibility problem. Production planning frequently "lives in one system, materials management in another, shop floor execution in a third," with slow, manual or broken data flow between them; plans get made but nobody systematically compares actual to plan or feeds variance learning back into planning assumptions. INDUSTRY SOURCE — NetSuite and Onepint, accessed 2026-09-02. LOW-MEDIUM confidence.
- **Business risks:** When planners cannot trust supplier/production ETAs, they build buffer inventory that ties up working capital; manufacturers without real-time visibility reportedly carry 18-24% more inventory than visibility-enabled peers. INDUSTRY SOURCE — iFactory (an MES/visibility vendor), "Supply Chain Visibility: Real-Time Tracking From Raw Material to Shipment," accessed 2026-09-02. LOW confidence (vendor-authored, figure not independently sourced or Malaysia-specific).
- **Emotional pressures:** Blamed for schedule misses that may originate from production-status information gaps rather than planning-process failures.
- **Questions and objections:** Whether a new system will actually close the loop between shop-floor execution and the planning system, versus becoming another disconnected data source.
- **Purchase motivations and triggers:** Real-time linkage of production/WIP status into the planning system without manual reconciliation, allowing feasible plans and faster response to schedule risk. INDUSTRY SOURCE — NetSuite, accessed 2026-09-02.
- **Rejection triggers:** Concern about integration complexity between MES and existing planning/MRP tools; skepticism if prior "visibility" tools didn't deliver.
- **Information needed before buying:** Concrete detail on how MES production/WIP data flows into planning without manual re-entry.

- **Content angles and hooks:** "Plan vs. actual" gap framing (already flagged as an important business concept in `01_BUSINESS/company_profile.md` §14) is a natural angle for this audience — not yet tested as content.
- **Language used / messages to avoid:** Lead with plan/schedule/WIP-accuracy language rather than general "visibility" abstractions.
