# Finance Manager

## Profile

_(Evidence-backed profile — first pass, external/industry research. Not yet validated against Mysoft's own sales or customer data. Last updated: 2026-09-02.)_

- **Role and responsibilities:** Cost control, production costing, ROI justification, budget ownership or influence. VERIFIED FACT (internal business knowledge, `01_BUSINESS/company_profile.md` §10-19), HIGH confidence.
- **KPIs:** Cost variance (actual vs. standard/budgeted), COGS accuracy, margin by product/line/customer, labour cost variance. INDUSTRY SOURCE — Triad ("How Manufacturing ERP Improves Production Cost Control," accessed 2026-09-02), OneAdvanced ("Manufacturing Accounting: How to Track Costs, Inventory and Profitability," accessed 2026-09-02). LOW-MEDIUM confidence (general international manufacturing-finance content, not Malaysia-specific).
- **Daily frustrations:** Production-cost information is spread across spreadsheets, accounting software, inventory systems and production records; finance can see that costs increased but not always why, or where. INDUSTRY SOURCE — Triad, accessed 2026-09-02. LOW-MEDIUM confidence.
- **Operational problems:** When ERP financial data updates weekly but shop-floor (MES) data updates continuously, finance and operations end up working from two different versions of "current" cost/margin reality — plant managers make scheduling calls from the MES view, finance makes pricing/forecasting calls from the ERP view, and the two can quietly diverge. INDUSTRY SOURCE — Kyle David Group, "How Manufacturers Can Achieve Real-Time Margin Visibility," accessed 2026-09-02 (manufacturing-ERP consultancy). MEDIUM confidence (industry-consultant analysis, not Malaysia-specific, not independently verified against Mysoft customers).
- **Business risks:** Internal tension between operations (pushing for higher output) and finance (concerned about unfavourable labour-cost absorption) when the two sides lack a shared, real-time data source. INDUSTRY SOURCE — SysGenPro, "Manufacturing ERP Reporting for Plant Performance and Margin Control," accessed 2026-09-02. LOW-MEDIUM confidence.
- **Questions and objections:** Likely to ask how MES data feeds financial reporting/costing rather than sitting as a separate operational system; may see MES as an operations-only tool with no direct finance benefit unless this link is made explicit.
- **Purchase motivations and triggers:** Real-time or near-real-time visibility into labour hours, material usage and machine time feeding directly into the ERP costing engine, turning contribution margin by SKU/line/customer into a live metric instead of a month-end calculation; reduced inventory drift through automated backflushing as units complete. INDUSTRY SOURCE — Kyle David Group, accessed 2026-09-02. MEDIUM confidence.
- **Rejection triggers:** Perceived duplication with existing ERP/accounting reporting; unclear ROI if the finance-facing benefit isn't demonstrated concretely.
- **Information needed before buying:** How MES production data maps into existing cost/margin reporting; what manual reconciliation work it removes.
- **Preferred content and platforms:** Not yet role-specific in existing platform research — see `02_AUDIENCE/audience_matrix.md` for company-wide findings; LinkedIn is currently the strongest indicated channel for business-case/ROI-style content aimed at non-production decision-influencers.
- **Content angles and hooks:** Cost-of-inaccurate-reporting angle ("what does a delayed/wrong production number cost in real margin terms") is a plausible hook not yet tested — HYPOTHESIS, not yet validated.
- **Language used / messages to avoid:** Lead with cost/margin/ROI language rather than shop-floor operational language; avoid assuming finance cares about OEE or downtime in isolation from their cost impact.
- **Funnel considerations:** Likely PRODUCT AWARE to HIGH INTENT during evaluation/approval stage rather than early awareness — finance is more often an approver/technical-financial evaluator than an initiator (per `01_BUSINESS/company_profile.md` §18-19 buying-role classification, not yet confirmed with real deal data).
- **Evidence and customer observations:** UNKNOWN — no direct Mysoft finance-manager customer interviews on file yet, and no Malaysia-specific source was found for this role this pass. Evidence gap: whether Malaysian manufacturing finance managers actually engage directly in MES evaluation, or whether this role only reviews the business case after production/ops has already selected a vendor, is unconfirmed.
