## 2026-08-31
LinkedIn is decisively the primary B2B research channel for industrial software buyers, and by 2026 has overtaken trade publications as the top research channel for industrial buyers under 45, with 85% of manufacturing marketers rating it their best-performing channel and manufacturing marketing budgets rebounding to 9.5% of revenue. The defining 2026 shift is founder/named-expert-led content over company pages: posts from a named CEO, head of engineering, or technical lead now get 6–10x the reach and engagement of company-page posts, and algorithm changes reward depth and genuine expertise signals over volume or engagement bait. For MES specifically, the highest-performing content types are technical, evidence-based, and buyer-journey-mapped: case studies with quantified outcomes (e.g. 'reduced changeover time by X%'), non-promotional technical breakdowns of a real production problem, and short (60–90s) founder-commentary videos or product-walkthrough clips solving one specific problem. Vertical/industry-specific SaaS positioning (vs. generic ERP/MES messaging) is outperforming horizontal platforms industry-wide, so leading with Malaysian-manufacturing-specific pain points (audits, Industry4WRD grants, multi-shift traceability) rather than generic 'digital transformation' language is likely to land better. Recommendation: 1 founder or technical lead publishing weekly, not just a company page.


## 2026-09-03 — Algorithm detail (external benchmark → TESTING, not Mysoft-measured)

Access date 2026-09-03. Sources: multiple 2026 B2B-marketing write-ups on the LinkedIn
algorithm (Brixon, SocialBee, Bang Marketing, Digital Applied, UseVisuals). Aggregated
marketer commentary, not category- or Malaysia-specific; directional only.

- **"Relevance over network" (reinforced by a March 2026 update).** Distribution is driven
  by topical match between the post and the author's stated profile expertise plus a
  distinctive point of view — off-topic or generic posts are throttled. Supports the
  existing recommendation: one founder / technical lead posting consistently on a
  consistent MES/manufacturing theme beats a scattered company page.
- **"Depth Score" — active dwell time is now a primary ranking signal**, over likes/reactions.
  Write for read-through, not for the reaction count.
- **Engagement-bait and external links reportedly down-ranked ~60%.** CTAs like "comment
  below for the PDF" / "tag someone" trigger a filter that caps the post at the author's
  first-degree network. **Implication for `05_CREATIVE/content_calendars/2026-09-linkedin.md`:**
  no "comment for the link" mechanics; put external links in the first comment, not the post
  body; let the hook carry reach, not a bait line.
- **Carousel / document posts reportedly generate 2–3× the dwell time of text or image
  posts** — strongest native format under the dwell-time regime. Worth testing a
  carousel format (e.g. "what a shift-handover looks like on paper vs one digital record")
  for the production_manager / GM audience alongside the founder-video track. Route the
  idea to `08_DECISIONS/content_backlog.md`, not a rule.
- **Status:** TESTING. Revisit against Mysoft first-party LinkedIn data once the September
  cycle produces any.
