## 2026-09-04 — RED/XHS Malaysia sizing + content style (external → TESTING; audience-fit caveat)

Access date 2026-09-04. Sources: Hashmeta "Complete Guide to Xiaohongshu Marketing in Malaysia"; Mayin Digital "Xiaohongshu Marketing Malaysia 2026"; MySense "XHS Marketing Malaysia 2026". Agency / marketing-vendor content, not MES- or B2B-industrial-specific — directional only.

- **Sizing (upgrades the earlier one-data-point note):** ~3.5M active XHS users in Malaysia, reported at ~40% of the ethnic-Chinese population; concentrated in KL, Penang, Johor Bahru.
- **Audience skew:** women ~18–35, above-average spending power. **This is a consumer-discovery audience, not the MES buying committee** (factory owners / GMs / production managers, 35–55, mostly male per `02_AUDIENCE/`). The mismatch is the key caveat: XHS reaches Chinese-Malaysian *urbanites*, not necessarily Chinese-Malaysian *manufacturers*.
- **Content style that works on RED:** users arrive with research / purchase intent; "honest review notes" and "practical guide notes" out-perform; polished brand announcements earn the fewest saves and enquiries. Educational, first-person, un-glossy.
- **So what for Mysoft:** the *Chinese-language factory-digitalisation education* white space is real and still uncontested by tracked competitors — but XHS is a **small, low-cost test at best**, and only for plain-language educational notes (e.g. "紙本工單的隱藏成本" / "what a digital job traveller actually records"), never a product announcement. A Chinese-language asset is likely better placed on Facebook (where the 35+ Chinese-Malaysian SME-owner audience actually is) first, with XHS as a secondary repost.
- **Status:** TESTING / MONITOR. Not a resourced channel. Route any test idea to `08_DECISIONS/content_backlog.md`.
