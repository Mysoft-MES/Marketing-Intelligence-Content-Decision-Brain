## 1. MEANING — what this research actually tells us about Mysoft

**Mysoft is content-silent in a market where its one tracked competitor, in the same city,
has already built the library.** Allied Solutions Global runs a complete, connected
buyer-education funnel — what-is-MES → MES-vs-ERP → buyer's guide → how-to-choose →
implementation guide → ROI/cost → web-based MES → OEE worked examples → OT cybersecurity —
plus a Penang office, KL office, regional SEO, trade-show presence (Penang Expo 22–24 Jul,
Johor Fair Aug), and global product proof (TrakSYS: Heineken, Pfizer, Nestlé logos). Mysoft
has **zero published assets**. On "explain MES well for this region," Mysoft is not behind —
it has not started.

**But Allied's library is generic, and built for the last era of distribution.** Its content
is English-only, corporate and unbylined, long-form article + webinar shaped, and it leads
with OEE / downtime / compliance / ERP-connectivity, increasingly dressed in "AI-driven
optimisation / IIoT / no-code / web-native" language. Every 2026 platform signal this run
confirmed points the other way:
- **LinkedIn** rewards named individuals over company pages (company-page reach −60–66%
  since 2024; employee posts cited ~+561% reach-per-post), document carousels over articles
  (~6.6% engagement, highest format), and dwell time ("Depth Score") over reach.
- **Instagram** now wants SEO-style keyword captions, not hashtag blocks, and rewards
  DM sends / shares / watch-through over likes.
- **Facebook** in Malaysia rewards localised, Malaysian-specific, often Mandarin creative —
  ~74% of Malaysian business leaders use Meta to discover solutions.
- **Xiaohongshu** rewards honest first-person "review notes," punishes brand announcements.

Allied is adapting to none of this. That is the opening.

**Mysoft's real asset is not feature parity — it is frame and proximity.** Mysoft cannot and
should not claim the breadth Allied's two platforms carry (`swot.md` §22 — do not pretend
feature parity). What Mysoft has that Allied structurally lacks:
1. **A different problem frame.** Allied asks "how is your OEE?" Mysoft asks *"can you verify
   what actually happened on the floor — or are you managing from the report of what
   happened?"* (`positioning.md` §32, `swot.md` §12). Allied does **not** lead with this.
2. **Proximity.** Same city (Penang), local language (Mandarin / BM), direct shop-floor
   access, and — once the owner allows it — a founder who can be on camera. Allied's content
   has no face and no local language.
3. **A "start small" product story.** Digital Job Order first, expand later — which now even
   matches where Allied's own supplier is heading (Parsec's Connected Worker, "a scalable
   path to full MES," May 2026), but Mysoft can tell it as a local, plain-language story
   rather than a platform brochure.

**The macro says "why now" is real and dated — but it is context, not a campaign.** Soft-but-
positive PMI (50.2 Aug, third month of growth), the foreign-labour squeeze pushing
automation, the Automation Capital Allowance sunsetting at YA2027, Budget 2027 on 9 October,
and "cloud MES lowers the SME barrier" all support a "is now the time to digitise the floor?"
angle. Use it as background timing, never as a Mysoft grant claim.

**The binding constraint is not ideas or evidence — it is four gates.** (1) No named
founder / technical lead for LinkedIn. (2) No customer proof — the quantified case study,
LinkedIn's strongest format, is unavailable until an implementation exists. (3)
`current_priorities.md` carries no owner-set business priority, so nothing can be ranked.
(4) Lean execution capacity (`company_profile.md` §35). Every strong move below is gated on
one of these, and saying so plainly is part of the brief.

| | Mysoft | Allied Solutions Global |

| Published content | **None** | Complete buyer-journey funnel, ~1–3 pieces/month, sustained through 2026 |

| Local language | Planned: Mandarin / BM | English only |
| Problem frame | "Verify what actually happened / plan vs actual" | OEE / downtime / compliance / ERP-connectivity |
| Register | Plain, shop-floor | Drifting to "AI-driven / IIoT / no-code" |

**Where we are weak:** everything published. No funnel, no proof, no SEO footprint, no data.

**Where the competitor is stronger:** buyer education at volume, regional search, product
maturity and breadth, global logos, trade-show reach, same-city sales presence.

**Where we can win — the white space Allied leaves open:**
- **Format** — LinkedIn document carousel and short vertical video. No Allied carousel or
  Reel exists.
- **Voice** — one named founder / technical lead. Allied has none; 2026 punishes that hard.
- **Language & locality** — Mandarin, Bahasa Malaysia, Penang-specific. Uncontested.
- **Problem frame** — "can management verify what actually happened?" / "the report says
  500, the floor made 480." Allied does not lead here.
- **Register** — stay plain and shop-floor while Allied leans on AI/IIoT vocabulary.

**The opportunity to take now** is *not* "out-publish Allied's guides." It is to occupy the
shape and the frame Allied cannot easily copy: run the September social cycle (already
built), ship the carousel, and start exactly one owned website explainer on the
verification frame. Close the buyer-education gap slowly, on Mysoft's own register — do not
race Allied on their track.

## 3. MARKETING DECISION

1. **Hold the position.** Keep the problem-first, plain-language, "production truth / verify
   what actually happened" frame. Do **not** adopt Allied's OEE / AI / IIoT / no-code
   vocabulary — the differentiation is partly linguistic and chasing it erases the edge.
2. **Ship the September social cycle as the first data-generating move.** LinkedIn text
   experiment (counterbalanced Tue/Thu — designed to produce Mysoft's first validated
   posting time), Facebook Click-to-WhatsApp Reels, Instagram process-breakdown Reels, plus
   LI-C1 carousel. The objective this cycle is **a first validated posting time and a first
   audience read**, not volume. All still `AWAITING HUMAN APPROVAL`.
3. **Open the buyer-education front with exactly one owned asset**, on Mysoft's frame: a
   mysoftmes.com explainer — *"MES vs your current process: can you verify what actually
   happened on the floor?"* — as the wedge against Allied's funnel. **Gated** on the owner
   (`current_priorities.md` item 2). No generation prompt until then.
4. **Named-person LinkedIn direction stays parked** until the owner names a person
   (`current_priorities.md` item 1). LI-C1 can still run as a company-page asset.
5. **Add Mandarin / Penang-local as the next distinct asset** once shop-floor footage
   exists — one Mandarin Facebook Reel, optional Xiaohongshu repost as a low-cost test only.
