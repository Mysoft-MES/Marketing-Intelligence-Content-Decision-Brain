# Creative Strategy

## Principles

- Lead with a recognizable audience problem before introducing the product when appropriate.
- Make the opening immediately useful; avoid unnecessary logo introductions.
- Adapt execution to platform, audience, objective, and funnel stage.
- Keep claims within verified product and business evidence.
- Treat every recommendation as a measurable hypothesis.
