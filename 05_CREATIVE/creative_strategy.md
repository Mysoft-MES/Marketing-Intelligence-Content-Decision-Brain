# Creative Strategy

Last updated: 2026-09-02

Canonical home for creative operating principles. `05_CREATIVE/creative_rules.md` was merged here on 2026-09-02 and retired — it was an empty placeholder covering the same ground.

## Principles

- Lead with a recognizable audience problem before introducing the product when appropriate.
- Make the opening immediately useful; avoid unnecessary logo introductions.
- Adapt execution to platform, audience, objective, and funnel stage.
- Keep claims within verified product and business evidence.
- Treat every recommendation as a measurable hypothesis.

## Execution Rules

Merged from `creative_rules.md`, 2026-09-02.

- Hook the viewer immediately — the first 1–3 seconds decide whether anything else is seen.
- Show the problem before the product where the audience is problem-aware.
- Do not overcrowd a short video with multiple messages. One idea per asset.
- Never publish identical content across platforms. Shared footage is fine; shared structure, length, hook and CTA are not (`00_SYSTEM/brain_rules.md` §7).
- Check every claim against `01_BUSINESS/products.md` §30 (Product Claim Safety) and §31 (Prohibited Unverified Claims) before production.
- Match CTA friction to funnel stage. Awareness content does not carry a demo ask.
- Where a figure or customer outcome is not on file, do not invent one — reframe the idea so it does not need one.
