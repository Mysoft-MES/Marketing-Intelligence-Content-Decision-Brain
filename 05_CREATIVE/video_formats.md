## Format — Process Breakdown Reel (proposed, 2026-09-02)

**Best use:** Walking through a specific operational moment or methodology step-by-step (e.g. shift-change handover, traceability check) rather than a single dramatized scenario.
**Suitable audience:** Production Manager (primary); may extend to Quality Manager, Plant Manager.
**Suitable platform:** Instagram Reels (secondary channel per `03_PLATFORM/instagram.md`); reusable on Facebook Reels per existing cross-posting pattern.
**Suggested duration:** ~60–90 seconds (per source case study in `07_RESEARCH/2026-09-02-instagram-content-opportunities-production-manager.md`; not yet tested against Mysoft's own retention data).
**Strength:** Differentiates from the existing single-scenario "where's Job #4471" hook (`05_CREATIVE/hook_library.md`) by showing a full step-by-step moment (e.g. shift handover) rather than one dramatized beat; can reuse footage from the same shop-floor shoot at low incremental cost.
**Weakness:** Evidence for this format's performance is a single non-Malaysia, non-MES case study (LOW-MEDIUM confidence, Tier 4 source) — not yet validated for Mysoft's own audience or category. Longer format (60–90s) risks lower completion rate than the existing shorter scenario hook if not tightly edited.
**Status:** PROPOSED — untested. Do not treat as a validated pattern; see `08_DECISIONS/content_backlog.md` for the routed opportunity entry and `00_SYSTEM/decision_framework.md` scoring.
