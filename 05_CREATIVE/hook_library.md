### CONTENT GENERATION PROMPT (for image/video generation tool)

**Format:** Short vertical video, 30–45 seconds, Facebook/Instagram Reels aspect ratio (9:16)

**Hook (first 1–3 seconds, on-screen text + visual):**
Open on a cluttered shop-floor office desk — a whiteboard covered in half-erased job numbers, a stack of paper Job Travellers, an Excel sheet open on a monitor with a visible red error cell. A hand taps the desk in mild frustration. On-screen text overlay, bold sans-serif: **"Where's Job #4471 right now?"** — a beat of silence, nobody answers.

**Hook type:** Problem / relatable situation (not curiosity-bait — this is a real, specific, everyday shop-floor moment, matching the "specificity over promotion" preference the source community rewards)

**Visual approach / shot list:**
1. (0–3s) Static desk shot as described above — muted, slightly desaturated color grade to feel "unglamorous" and real, not a stock-photo factory.
2. (3–8s) Quick cuts: supervisor walking the floor asking two different operators "where's this job?" and getting two different, uncertain answers (visualize as two brief handheld-style clips, natural factory lighting, no dramatic music sting — keep it grounded).
3. (8–15s) Cut to a tablet or shop-floor terminal screen lighting up — simple, uncluttered UI showing a single job card: status, current station, operator name, timestamp. No dashboard overload, no "smart factory" buzzword graphics — just the one answer to the one question.
4. (15–25s) Text overlay: **"You don't need a new ERP. You need one clear answer."** Show the same supervisor glancing at the screen, immediately walking toward the right station — visibly relieved, not triumphant (avoid over-acted "wow" reactions).
5. (25–35s) Product moment: brief, clean shot of the Digital Job Traveller interface on a tablet mounted shop-side. Keep this understated — a tool, not a spectacle.
6. (35–45s) End card: Mysoft MES logo, tagline text "看见每一步，掌握每一程" (small, secondary), and CTA text: **"See where every job actually is. Message us."**

**Tone:** Plain-spoken, credible, slightly self-aware about vendor fatigue — closer to a shop-floor colleague explaining something than a software ad. No orchestral/corporate stock music; if music is used, keep it low-key and functional (light percussion, no swelling strings). No actors in suits; wardrobe should read as real factory floor (safety vests, work shirts).

**CTA:** Click-to-WhatsApp ("Message us") per Facebook platform notes — low-to-medium intent CTA appropriate for a Problem Aware audience, not "Book a Demo."

**Why this can surpass existing category content:** Competitor and category content in this space defaults to feature lists and "smart factory" imagery. This creative deliberately underplays the technology and leads with the single-question moment — matching the specific, evidence-based preference (simple single-purpose visibility, not full digital transformation) found in the Reddit/Practical Machinist research, which no local competitor's content currently reflects.

**Secondary metric:** Comments/shares indicating recognition ("this is literally my shop") — a qualitative signal of message-market fit before scaling spend
