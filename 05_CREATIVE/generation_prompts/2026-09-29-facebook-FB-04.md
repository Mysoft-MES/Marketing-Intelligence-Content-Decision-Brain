## Hook (first 1–3 seconds)
A manager holding a production report, calm, to camera: "Nobody's lying. The record just can't be checked." Hook type: Contrarian.

## Shot list / composition
1. 0–4s: Mid-shot, manager on the floor, even tone, delivering the hook line. No tension music.
2. 4–16s: Show *why* a paper report resists checking — consolidated by hand from five station sheets, one number reaching the office, the trail already cold. Neutral observation, not accusation.
3. 16–30s: The same question against a structured digital record — the query lands on the specific entry: which station, which operator, what time. Framed as "now we can look", not "now we caught someone".
4. 30–40s: VO: traceability is about being able to check, not about blame.
5. 40–45s: End card + WhatsApp CTA.

## On-screen text
- Hook: "Nobody's lying. The record just can't be checked."
- Mid: "Traceability ≠ blame"
- End: "Ask what a checkable production record looks like → WhatsApp"

## Tone and art direction
Deliberately de-dramatised — calm VO, neutral faces, even light throughout (no cool/warm morality split here). The message is trust-building. Real floor, real report paperwork.

## What must NOT appear
- Do not promise elimination of error or 100% accuracy (`products.md` §20, §31).
- No implication of dishonest staff — the hook exists to defuse that read; keep visuals consistent with it (`positioning.md` §5).
- No % / ROI / savings / implementation-time claims (`products.md` §31).
- No Digiwin upload mechanism depiction (`products.md` §7).
- Do not present total/automatic traceability as a checkbox — a digital record only traces what it was configured to capture (`products.md` §19).

## CTA
