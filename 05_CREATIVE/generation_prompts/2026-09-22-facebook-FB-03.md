## Evidence basis
- Research file: `01_BUSINESS/company_profile.md` §31 — 「看不见的损失，才是最昂贵的损失。」 and 「从经验管理，到数据管理。」 are existing brand assets whose effectiveness has **not been measured** and which should not be used by default.
- This asset is therefore a **message test as much as a timing test** — see calendar note.
- Evidence tier and confidence: brand lines are internal assets, unproven; `LOW` / `TEST`.

## Hook (first 1–3 seconds)
Black frame, Mandarin VO + text: 「看不见的损失，才是最昂贵的损失。」 ("The loss you can't see is the most expensive loss.") Hook type: Fear / Risk.

## Shot list / composition
1. 0–3s: Near-black. The line appears in Mandarin, spoken quietly.
2. 3–14s: "Before" — a real floor run on experience and memory: verbal handovers, a half-updated whiteboard, a supervisor reconstructing the day from notes at 6pm. Cool, slightly dim.
3. 14–28s: Transition on the second line 「从经验管理，到数据管理。」 — same floor, same people, now logging at the station as they go; a structured record building up through the shift.
4. 28–40s: A manager reviews that record calmly instead of chasing people. VO ties "unseen loss" to decisions made on stale information.
5. 40–45s: End card + WhatsApp CTA.

## On-screen text
- Hook: 「看不见的损失，才是最昂贵的损失。」 (+ EN subtitle)
- Turn: 「从经验管理，到数据管理。」 (+ EN subtitle)
- End: "Message us to see what a data-driven floor record looks like → WhatsApp"

## Tone and art direction
Reflective, respectful of experienced operators — the "before" is not incompetence, it's invisibility. Mandarin VO unhurried. Warm up the grade after the turn. No alarmist music.

## What must NOT appear
- Do not quantify the "loss" — no RM figures, no % waste, no ROI (`products.md` §31).
- No "eliminates" / "guarantees" / zero-error claims (`products.md` §20, §31).
- No Digiwin upload mechanism depiction (`products.md` §7).
- Do not imply staff are hiding losses on purpose (`positioning.md` §5).
- Brand lines are unproven — the asset tests them, so do not also assert they are proven.

## CTA
Click-to-WhatsApp — "Message us".
