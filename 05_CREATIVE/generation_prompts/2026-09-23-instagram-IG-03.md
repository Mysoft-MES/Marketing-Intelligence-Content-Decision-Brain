## Evidence basis

## Hook (first 1–3 seconds)
Text over a shot of a phone showing a customer message: "Customer asks what happened on job 4471 last Tuesday. Go." Hook type: Scenario.

## Shot list / composition
A step-by-step "traceability check" performed on screen:
1. 0–4s: The customer question, hook text.
2. Step 1 (≈12s): Open job 4471. Caption: "Start at the job, not the person."
3. Step 2 (≈15s): Walk the history entries in order — received → station A → station B → QC → done — each with its timestamp and operator. Caption per step.
4. Step 3 (≈15s): Land on the point in the sequence where the question actually is (e.g. a gap between two timestamps). Caption: "Here's the hour in question. Here's who was on it."
5. Step 4 (≈15s): Compose the factual answer back to the customer from what the record shows. Caption: "Answer = what the record captured. Nothing more, nothing less."
6. 80–90s: End text: "Save this."

## On-screen text
- Hook line
- Step captions (as above)
- "Only what the record captured"
- End: "Save this."

## Tone and art direction
Procedural, confident, unhurried — a competent manager doing a normal task well. Real UI (or accurately labelled mock). Light, functional captions. No dramatic music.

## What must NOT appear
- Do not imply the system can reconstruct anything that was not configured to be captured — explicitly cap the answer at "what the record captured" (`products.md` §19).
- No "complete traceability" / "full audit trail" / regulatory-compliance claims (`products.md` §19, §31).
- No Digiwin upload depiction (`products.md` §7).
- No blame framing toward the operator on shift (`positioning.md` §5).
- Dummy data only — no real customer or job data.

## CTA
"Save this." No hard CTA.
