## Hook (first 1–3 seconds)
Split screen, same job number on both sides: left a paper Job Traveller, right a digital Job Order record. Caption: "Same job. Two records. Only one can be checked." Hook type: Comparison.

## Shot list / composition
1. 0–3s: Hard split screen. Left: hand writing on a paper traveller. Right: the same job appearing as a structured digital record. Job number identical and legible on both.
2. 3–15s: Left side accumulates friction — a crossed-out quantity, a missing time, a coffee ring, ambiguous initials. Right side stays clean: station, operator, timestamp, quantity as discrete fields.
3. 15–30s: A "can you check this?" moment — a hand tries to trace the paper record and stalls; the same query on the digital record jumps straight to the entry with who/when.
4. 30–40s: VO: the point isn't paper vs screen — it's whether the record can be checked later.
5. 40–45s: End card + WhatsApp CTA.
