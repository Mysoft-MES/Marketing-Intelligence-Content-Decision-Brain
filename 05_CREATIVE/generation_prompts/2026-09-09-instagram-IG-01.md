## Hook (first 1–3 seconds)
Text on frame over a wide shot of a production line: "Six stations. Five handwritten records. One number reaches your office." Hook type: Problem.

## Shot list / composition
Station-by-station structure, one clean segment per station, consistent framing and lower-third labels ("STATION 1 / 2 / …"):
1. 0–5s: Hook text over the full line.
2. Station 1–5 (≈10s each): at each, a real operator records by hand — a tally, a time, a quantity. Each segment shows one small, believable way the record drifts: a delayed entry, a rounded quantity, an ambiguous initial, a skipped field, a note written from memory at break.
3. Final station / consolidation (≈10s): a supervisor adds the sheets together into a single figure for the daily report.
4. 75–90s: Freeze on that single number with the caption "This is the number your decisions are based on." Save prompt.

## On-screen text
- Hook line (as above)
- Per station: the specific drift ("Entry written 40 min later", "Quantity rounded", "Whose initials?", "Field left blank", "Filled in from memory")
- End: "Save this if it's your floor."

## Tone and art direction
Observational, quiet, respectful of operators — the drift is normal human behaviour under time pressure, not negligence. Consistent grade, steady handheld, natural sound of the floor. No music sting, no VO required. Legible text for sound-off.

## Caption (publish copy) — DRAFT
