## Hook (first 1–3 seconds)
Text over a single clean job record on screen: "One job. One screen. Station, operator, timestamp." Hook type: Demonstration.

## Shot list / composition
Screen-only, no hands, no face:
1. 0–3s: The job record, hook text overlaid.
2. 3–20s: Slow, deliberate cursor tour of the record's real fields — job number, current station, operator, timestamp, quantity, status. Each field gets a 2–3s hold with a short caption naming it.
3. 20–40s: Show the *history* view of that one job — the sequence of entries as it moved between stations. Caption: "Every entry is stamped with who and when."
4. 40–55s: Pull back to the job list; the one job highlighted. Caption: "This is a floor walk you can do from your desk."
5. 55–60s: End text: "Save for your next floor walk."
