## Hook (first 1–3 seconds)
On-floor, a boss asks 「这批货做到哪里了？」 ("Where is this batch up to?") — then a beat of silence as three staff look at each other. Hook type: Problem / relatable situation.

## Shot list / composition
1. 0–3s: Handheld, over-the-shoulder of a factory owner (smart casual, 45–55) on a real shop floor asking the question. Ambient machine noise. Freeze + subtle zoom on the silence.
2. 3–12s: Quick cuts of the "answer hunt" — a supervisor walking the floor, flipping a clipboard, phoning someone, squinting at a whiteboard. Slightly desaturated, mildly tense pacing.
3. 12–25s: Slow push-in on a paper Job Traveller with smudged handwriting and a blank field. VO names the real cost: decisions get made before the floor answer arrives.
4. 25–38s: Calm tonal shift — warmer light. A tablet on the floor showing a structured digital job record (station, operator, timestamp) held next to the same job. Neutral, not triumphant.
5. 38–45s: End card — brand mark, one line, WhatsApp CTA.

## On-screen text
- Hook line (MY/EN subtitle of the Mandarin question)
- Mid: "The floor knows. The office finds out later."
- End: "See what a digital job record looks like → Message us on WhatsApp"

## Tone and art direction
Documentary, real factory, natural light, no stock-office gloss. Mandarin VO calm and plain, not salesy. Colour: neutral-cool for the "problem" half, a half-stop warmer for the "digital record" half. No dramatised conflict, no blaming staff.

## What must NOT appear
- No claim that the system eliminates errors, guarantees accurate data, or gives "100% visibility" (`products.md` §20, §31).
- No percentage improvements, ROI, cost-savings figures, or implementation timelines (`products.md` §31).
- Do not depict or narrate the Digiwin AIoT upload mechanism — fields, method, frequency are TO VERIFY (`products.md` §7).
- The tablet UI must be a clearly representative mock, not passed off as a specific shipping screen, unless a real Mysoft screen capture is supplied.
- No implication that employees are lying (`positioning.md` §5).

## CTA
Click-to-WhatsApp — "Message us".

## Hypothesis
WE BELIEVE opening on the recognisable "where is this job" silence FOR General Managers / owners ON Facebook WILL earn above-baseline 3-second retention and WhatsApp conversations BECAUSE the moment is a lived, unresolved pain for the decision-maker and no local competitor is dramatising it.
