## Hook (first 1–3 seconds)
On-screen text (large, bold simplified Chinese): **"这张工单，昨天到底做了多少?"**
("This job order — how much did it actually make yesterday?")
Visual: a boss's hand picking up a worn paper job traveller from a cluttered shop-floor desk
— handwriting, a couple of blank boxes, a smudge. A beat of silence. Nobody answers.
Hook type: problem / relatable situation — a specific, everyday moment, not curiosity bait.

## Shot list / composition
1. **(0–3s)** Desk shot as above — muted, slightly desaturated, real (not a stock-photo
   factory). The paper traveller in hand.
2. **(3–10s)** Quick cuts: the owner walks the floor, asks two workers "这单做到哪里了?"
   ("where is this job up to?"), gets two different, uncertain answers. Handheld, natural
   factory light, no dramatic music.
3. **(10–18s)** On-screen text: **"答案在三个地方 — 只有一个留得下来。"** ("The answer is in
   three places — only one of them lasts.") Visual: three quick images — someone talking, a
   half-filled paper line, an empty chair at shift end.
4. **(18–28s)** Cut to a shop-floor tablet / terminal showing one simple job record card
   (LABELLED 示意图 / "illustrative"): job, step, worker name, time, quantity entered. No
   dashboard overload, no smart-factory graphics — one clear answer to the one question.
5. **(28–38s)** On-screen text: **"看得见每一步，才管得住每一程。"** (existing brand line —
   "see every step, control every process".) The owner glances at the screen, then walks
   straight to the right station — relieved, not triumphant.
6. **(38–45s)** End card: Mysoft MES logo; small secondary line 从经验管理，到数据管理;
   CTA text: **"想知道你的车间实际发生了什么? 私讯我们。"** ("Want to know what actually
   happened on your floor? Message us.")

## On-screen text
All on-screen text in simplified Chinese, Malaysian Mandarin register (not Mainland
phrasing). Burned-in captions for the spoken lines (silent autoplay). English subtitle
track optional as a secondary version — not on this asset.

## Tone and art direction
Plain-spoken, credible, a little self-aware about vendor fatigue — a fellow factory owner
explaining something, not a software advertisement. Wardrobe reads as real factory floor
(polo shirts, safety vests) — no suits. No swelling strings; light functional percussion or
none. Real Malaysian SME factory setting — CNC / plastic injection / precision assembly, not
a spotless showroom.

## What must NOT appear
- No percentage figures, no "减少X%", no "零误差", no "100%准确", no guaranteed savings /
  ROI, no "任何ERP都能对接", no implementation-duration claims (`01_BUSINESS/products.md`
  §30–31).
- Do not depict or describe the Digiwin AIoT upload mechanism — TO VERIFY (`products.md` §7).
- No regulatory / audit-compliance outcome claims (`products.md` §19, §31).
- Traceability language capped at "记录了什么" ("what was recorded") — who / step / time /
  quantity as *entered*, not "全程追溯" as a guarantee or "证据".
- The record card is labelled 示意图 (illustrative) — not presented as a real Mysoft MES
  screenshot until a real capture is supplied.
- No competitor names. No "不像其他系统" framing — this is a problem story, not a comparison.
- No claim that Mysoft MES qualifies for any named government grant / incentive.

## CTA
Click-to-WhatsApp: **"想知道你的车间实际发生了什么? 私讯我们。"** — low-to-medium intent,
appropriate for a problem-aware owner audience. Not "预约演示" / "Book a demo".

## Hypothesis
WE BELIEVE a 30–45s Mandarin, owner-POV Reel built on the paper-job-traveller frame FOR
Chinese-Malaysian factory owners ON Facebook (paid, Click-to-WhatsApp) WILL earn a higher
click-to-WhatsApp rate and more "这就是我工厂" recognition comments than the English FB
Reels (FB-01…04) BECAUSE Mandarin creative is documented to build trust with this specific
owner segment, localised Malaysian creative converts better than generic assets, and no
competitor — including the one with a Penang office — publishes any Mandarin MES content.

## Success metric
Primary: Click-to-WhatsApp rate (link clicks / impressions on the paid placement) vs the
English FB Reels FB-01…04 once they have run.
Secondary: recognition comments ("这就是我工厂" / "我们也是这样"), WhatsApp conversations
started, cost per WhatsApp conversation vs the English Reels.
Comparison baseline: FB-01…04. No numeric target until a Mysoft Facebook baseline exists
(`decision_framework.md` §26).
