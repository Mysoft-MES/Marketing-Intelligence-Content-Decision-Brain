# IG-04 — Instagram — 2026-09-30

**Status:** DRAFT
**Calendar row:** 05_CREATIVE/content_calendars/2026-09-instagram.md → IG-04
**Platform:** Instagram (Reel)
**Audience:** Production Manager
**Objective / funnel stage:** Awareness → Solution Aware
**Asset type:** video
**Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical
**Target duration:** 60–90s
**Language:** English text-on-screen; minimal VO

## Evidence basis
- Research file: `01_BUSINESS/company_profile.md` §6 and `01_BUSINESS/products.md` §21 — paperless production is a listed solution area; the deeper value is faster information, better access, structured data, traceability, less manual handling — **not paperless for its own sake**.
- Evidence tier and confidence: solution area listed; station-level specifics TO VERIFY; `LOW` / `TEST`.

## Hook (first 1–3 seconds)
Close-up of a clipboard being set down; text: "Station 1: no more clipboard. Here's what replaces it." Hook type: Demonstration.

## Shot list / composition
Station-by-station "what changes here" structure, mirroring IG-01's format but solution-side:
1. 0–4s: The clipboard-down hook.
2. Station 1–4 (≈15s each): at each station, show the specific manual step that goes away and the structured digital entry that replaces it — scan/select job, confirm station, enter quantity, mark status. Caption names the before and the after per station.
3. Closing (≈10s): pull to the job record now assembled from those entries. Caption: "Same information — but structured, stamped, and retrievable."
4. 80–90s: End text: "Save this."

## On-screen text
- Hook line
- Per station: "Before: [manual step] → After: [structured entry]"
- Close: "Structured · stamped · retrievable"
- End: "Save this."

## Tone and art direction
Practical, grounded, shot on a real floor with real stations. The framing is "less manual handling and a better record", never "paper is bad". Consistent captions and grade. No hype music.

## What must NOT appear
- Do not frame paperless as the goal — frame it as faster/structured/retrievable information (`products.md` §21).
- Do not show data-capture methods that are not verified (scanning, machine/IIoT capture are TO VERIFY unless confirmed — `products.md` §35).
- No Digiwin upload depiction (`products.md` §7).
- No % improvement, time-saved, headcount, or ROI claims (`products.md` §31).
- Dummy job/operator data only.

## CTA
"Save this." No hard CTA.

## Hypothesis
WE BELIEVE a station-by-station "what replaces the clipboard" breakdown FOR production managers ON Instagram WILL earn watch-through and saves BECAUSE it answers the concrete "what actually changes on my floor" question that abstract digitalisation content skips.

## Success metric
Primary: watch-through rate.
Secondary: saves; reach. Compare format performance against IG-01 (same structure, problem-side).
