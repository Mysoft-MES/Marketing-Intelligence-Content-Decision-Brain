# WEBSITE CONTENT BENCHMARK — SEO + AEO + GEO + CONVERSION

Business: Mysoft MES

Company: My Software Solutions

Document Type: Content Scoring Standard

Status: Standard marking scheme for reviewing Mysoft MES pages

---

## PURPOSE

This is a repeatable, evidence-based benchmark for scoring Mysoft MES content — used instead of subjective ratings like "8/10."

When asked to "benchmark this page," the Brain should return a score broken down by category, followed by exactly which points were lost and what changes would recover them — not a vague verdict like "SEO is good."

---

## SCORING MODEL — 100 POINTS

### SEO — 30 points

| Assessment criterion | Points | What earns full marks |
|---|---:|---|
| Search intent | 5 | Page clearly satisfies the intended search query |
| Primary keyword targeting | 5 | Clear primary topic in title/H1/opening without stuffing |
| Semantic/topic coverage | 5 | Covers relevant terminology, problems and related concepts naturally |
| Title, H1 & headings | 4 | Logical hierarchy and descriptive headings |
| Internal linking | 4 | Links naturally to relevant solution/resource/conversion pages |
| Local relevance | 3 | Malaysia/Penang context where genuinely relevant |
| Originality/value | 4 | Offers information beyond generic MES explanations |

### AEO — 20 points

| Assessment criterion | Points | What earns full marks |
|---|---:|---|
| Direct answers | 5 | Important questions receive concise, self-contained answers |
| Question coverage | 4 | Addresses real questions prospects/searchers ask |
| Extractability | 4 | Answers can be understood without needing surrounding paragraphs |
| Structure | 3 | Headings, lists, definitions and FAQ make information easy to parse |
| Terminology clarity | 4 | Acronyms and technical concepts are clearly defined |

### GEO — 25 points

| Assessment criterion | Points | What earns full marks |
|---|---:|---|
| Entity clarity | 5 | Clear relationship between Mysoft MES, company, product, location and subject |
| First-party evidence | 5 | Real events, demonstrations, data, photos, experience or implementation knowledge |
| Verifiable claims | 4 | Claims are specific and supportable rather than generic marketing statements |
| Expertise/depth | 4 | Demonstrates actual manufacturing/MES knowledge |
| External corroboration potential | 3 | Important entities/facts can be validated elsewhere |
| Consistency | 4 | Product/company terminology and claims match other owned/external sources |

### Conversion — 15 points

| Assessment criterion | Points | What earns full marks |
|---|---:|---|
| CTA clarity | 4 | Visitor immediately understands the next action |
| Intent-to-CTA match | 3 | CTA makes sense for the visitor's stage |
| Trust | 3 | Evidence reduces uncertainty before asking for conversion |
| Value proposition | 3 | Explains why the visitor should choose/contact you |
| CTA placement | 2 | CTA appears at appropriate decision points |

### UX / Content Quality — 10 points

| Assessment criterion | Points | What earns full marks |
|---|---:|---|
| Scannability | 3 | Easy to skim |
| Conciseness | 2 | No unnecessary sections/repetition |
| Visual support | 2 | Images/video/diagrams support the information |
| Mobile readability | 2 | Paragraphs/headings work well on mobile |
| Language quality | 1 | Natural, professional writing |

**TOTAL: 100 points**

## SCORE INTERPRETATION

| Score | Grade | Meaning | Action |
|---:|:---:|---|---|
| 90–100 | A | Excellent | Publish / maintain |
| 80–89 | B | Strong | Minor optimization |
| 70–79 | C | Acceptable | Improve important weaknesses |
| 60–69 | D | Weak | Significant optimization needed |
| <60 | F | Poor | Rework page strategy/content |

---

## CRITICAL RULE — POINTS ARE NOT AWARDED FOR KEYWORD PRESENCE

SEO/AEO/GEO points cannot be awarded simply because a keyword appears.

**Example — does NOT earn GEO evidence points, despite containing "Mysoft MES," "MES," and "Malaysia":**

"Mysoft MES is the best MES solution in Malaysia."

**Example — DOES earn GEO evidence points, because it creates identifiable entity + date + location + activity + topic relationships that could be corroborated (event photos, social posts, venue information, partner posts):**

"On 20 August 2026, My Software Solutions hosted an MES Smart Manufacturing Workshop at Amari SPICE Penang focused on shop-floor digitalisation."

This rule exists to stop the Brain from optimizing for keyword density instead of information quality.

---

## SEPARATE TECHNICAL SEO CHECK

Technical SEO is NOT mixed into the content score above — otherwise a well-written article gets marked down for something the copywriter cannot control.

Use a separate **Pass / Warning / Fail** checklist covering:

- Indexability
- Canonical tags
- Meta title/description
- Schema markup
- Core Web Vitals
- Mobile responsiveness
- Image alt text
- Sitemap inclusion
- Broken links
- HTTPS
- Crawlability
- GA4 / Search Console tracking

**SEO [x]/30 | AEO [x]/20 | GEO [x]/25 | Conversion [x]/15 | UX [x]/10**

Followed by: exactly where points were lost, and what specific changes would recover them.
