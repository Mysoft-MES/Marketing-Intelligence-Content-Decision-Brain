## POSITIONING / BUSINESS MODEL

SOFtronix is an electronics-manufacturing-focused software vendor — its core original business is EDA (Electronics Design Automation) and CAM (Computer Aided Manufacturing) software for PCB assembly (PCBA) new-product-introduction (NPI), not MES. MES/MOM (Manufacturing Operation Management) was added as a product line in 2017, positioned alongside NPI tools like SMT placement programming, stencil design, and work-instruction software — i.e., MES is one module in a broader digital-PCBA-manufacturing toolkit, not a standalone flagship product with its own named brand.

## COMPETITIVE RELEVANCE TO MYSOFT MES

Narrower industry focus than Mysoft MES: SOFtronix appears specifically built around electronics/PCBA manufacturers (SMT lines, PCB assembly), one of Mysoft's named target industries. A manufacturer already using SOFtronix's EDA/CAM/NPI tools may have a natural path to also adopt its MES module (cross-sell advantage SOFtronix has that Mysoft would need to compete against specifically in the electronics/PCBA segment).
