## POSITIONING / BUSINESS MODEL — MES IS A MINOR MODULE WITHIN A GENERAL CUSTOM-SOFTWARE SHOP

Zoomo Tech is fundamentally a general custom software / mobile app development agency for startups and small businesses — its broader portfolio spans mobile apps, company websites, MLM (multi-level-marketing) system software, membership management software, and GPS tracking, in addition to "ERP system solutions featuring modules like MRP, MES, accounting software, and automated weighing systems." There is no named MES product/brand, and no MES-specific case study was found — MES is one FAQ-answered service line among many unrelated business-software offerings, a similar thin pattern to XTS Technologies and Regaltech.

## COMPETITIVE RELEVANCE TO MYSOFT MES

Low, but not zero — Zoomo Tech explicitly targets the same "Excel-driven SME" pain point Mysoft MES addresses ("helping them transition from Excel-driven processes to more efficient systems"), and frames its MES offering as affordable and SME-specific. However, as a generalist custom-software shop without manufacturing-sector specialization, it likely competes on price/customization for very small manufacturers rather than on manufacturing-specific depth.
