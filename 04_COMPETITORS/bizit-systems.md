## VERIFICATION

Bizit Systems' own site (bizits.com) describes i-MES as "an Industry 4.0 shop floor online Manufacturing Execution System for smart factory," offering "end-to-end wireless network of manufacturing workflow solution." The company publicly self-describes as "the leading Manufacturing Execution System in Malaysia and Singapore." This is a Tier 3 source (competitor official website) — confirms the company markets and sells MES software. Their own marketing claim of being "the leading" MES vendor is a COMPETITOR CLAIM, not independently verified — store as claim, not fact.

## POSITIONING / BUSINESS MODEL

Bizit is not a pure-play MES vendor. It also distributes Universal Robots, OnRobot and Epson Robots (industrial automation/cobots) and Minitab (statistical/quality software). MES appears to be bundled as one part of a broader automation + robotics + analytics offering rather than the company's sole focus. This bundling is itself a competitive angle — manufacturers wanting one vendor for both shop-floor software and robotics hardware.

## GEOGRAPHIC RELEVANCE

Johor Bahru / Kuala Lumpur / Singapore focus — south/central Malaysia, not directly overlapping Mysoft MES's core Penang/Northern Malaysia geography, but a national player that could compete for Malaysia-wide accounts.
