## IDENTITY

Company: FSBM MES Elite Sdn Bhd (FMES)
Parent: FSBM Holdings Berhad — publicly listed on Bursa Malaysia Main Market (stock code 9377) since 1994
Product: MES Elite, plus a lower-tier "MES Lite" starter package
Founded (MES business line): 2016
Registration verified via CTOS company record (reg. no. 0478625D / 199901003725)

## VERIFICATION

Multiple independent Tier 3/4 sources confirm this is a real, actively operating MES vendor:
- Company's own site (fsbmmes.com) describes MES, OEE, Predictive Maintenance, WIP tracking, ERP integration as core offerings, developed with "100% in-house software" by a local team.
- News coverage (The Sun, The Edge Malaysia) confirms a real product launch: "MES Lite" — a RM28,888 smart-factory starter package aimed at Malaysian SME manufacturers — plus a formal MoU with Selangor Human Resource Development Centre (SHRDC) for smart-manufacturing R&D, and government-linked project work (SIRIM-appointed IoT/MES/OEE rollout for 2 metalworking factories + 1 F&B factory).
- CTOS (credit reporting agency) company record independently confirms FSBM MES Elite Sdn Bhd's registered business activity is "PROVISION OF DEVELOPMENT IN MANUFACTURING EXECUTION SYSTEMS (MES) SOFTWARE AND SYSTEM INTEGRATION SERVICES."

This is one of the highest-confidence competitors found: a publicly-listed parent company, a dedicated MES subsidiary, government partnerships, and a named price point — all independently corroborated (not just self-reported).

## POSITIONING / BUSINESS MODEL

Positions as a "technical and process-oriented Digital System Integrator" — not just software, but end-to-end automation (MES + OEE + Predictive Maintenance + Vision Inspection + ERP integration + IIoT). Actively pursuing the low-end/SME segment with a named, publicly-quoted price (RM28,888 "MES Lite") — a pricing signal we do not have for most other competitors. Track record includes rubber-glove manufacturers (Malaysia + Thailand) and government-fund-supported SME rollouts (Industry4WRD Intervention Fund).

## GEOGRAPHIC RELEVANCE

Headquartered Kuala Lumpur/Shah Alam area — national reach, not Penang-specific, but as a Bursa-listed company with government relationships it likely has the resources to pursue accounts nationally, including Northern Malaysia.

## COMPETITIVE IMPLICATIONS FOR MYSOFT MES

- FSBM's published RM28,888 entry price point is a rare piece of real pricing evidence in this market — worth using as a reference point for Mysoft's own pricing/packaging conversations (see 07_RESEARCH entry).
- Being publicly listed gives FSBM credibility/marketing budget most other local competitors lack.
- Its SME/starter-package angle targets a different buyer (small manufacturer, price-sensitive, fast implementation) than a full custom MES engagement — a possible segment to watch.
