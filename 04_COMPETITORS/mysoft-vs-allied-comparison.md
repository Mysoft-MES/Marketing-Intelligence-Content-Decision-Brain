# Mysoft MES vs Allied Solutions Global / ASPL — Head-to-Head

**What this is.** A direct comparison between Mysoft MES and the one tracked competitor,
Allied Solutions Global / ASPL, built from `04_COMPETITORS/allied-solutions-global.md` (100%
human-supplied, verbatim), `01_BUSINESS/` (company_profile / products / positioning / swot /
sales_insights), `03_PLATFORM/`, and this run's research. Kept separate from
`allied-solutions-global.md` so that file stays verbatim.

**Last updated:** 2026-09-04.

**Confidence: LOW-MEDIUM.** Allied's product/positioning facts are well-sourced (their own
site); Allied's social/content *performance* is unmeasured (logged-in pass outstanding, 5
runs overdue); Allied's ROI figures and customer logos are **vendor claims, not verified
fact** (`00_SYSTEM/evidence_rules.md`). Mysoft's side is pre-revenue with zero first-party
data — every "strength" below is a capability, not a proven advantage
(`01_BUSINESS/sales_insights.md`). This is a planning tool, not a scorecard.

## 2. Where Allied is stronger (be honest)

1. **They have published; Mysoft has not.** A complete, connected, live buyer-journey
   library, sustained ~1–3 pieces/month through 2026, region-targeted for SEO.
2. **Product maturity and breadth.** Two established MES/MOM platforms covering OEE,
   downtime, quality/SPC, traceability/genealogy, electronic batch records, predictive
   maintenance, ERP/SCADA/Historian integration. Mysoft's proprietary MES capability is
   still developing (`swot.md` §22).
3. **Proof assets exist** (even if vendor-level) — global customer logos, worked examples,
   ROI framing. Mysoft cannot run the quantified case study until it has a customer.
4. **Reach:** trade shows (Penang Expo Jul 2026, Johor Fair Aug 2026), webinars with
   partners, regional search footprint.
5. **Same-city sales presence** — a Penang office competing for the same local deals.
6. **Backing:** named Siemens FA/Digital partner; Parsec's platform roadmap (e.g. Connected
   Worker, May 2026) evolves without Allied having to build it.

## 4. Where NOT to compete

- **Feature-checklist comparisons.** Mysoft does not have TrakSYS/Proficy breadth and should
  not pretend to (`swot.md` §22, §41). Compete on fit to a specific problem, not on a
  feature grid.
- **Re-publishing Allied's guides later.** Allied owns "explain MES for the region." A
  Mysoft what-is-MES / OEE / ROI guide published in 2026 is a follower move. If Mysoft builds
  owned buyer-education (REC-6), it is on the **verification frame** and its own register.
- **Price.** If Mysoft competes only on price, margin and differentiation both weaken
  (`swot.md` §38). Subsidised competitors (MDEC SmartMFG+ recipients) can go lower.
- **"AI / IIoT / smart factory" language.** Chasing it erases the plain-language edge.
- **Global-logo credibility.** Mysoft cannot match it and should not try; lead with the
  problem and the concept until a local implementation exists.

## 5. The one-line summary

> Allied is a competent regional distributor that has already built the MES buyer-education
> library for this market — generic, English-only, corporate-unbylined, long-form, and built
> for the last era of distribution. Mysoft cannot out-publish that funnel and should not try.
> Mysoft's opening is **shape** (named person, carousel, short-form, Mandarin, Penang-local)
> and **frame** ("can you verify what actually happened?" vs "how is your OEE?"), executed
> from a same-city, plain-language, shop-floor position — provided the owner names a person,
> the September cycle ships, and the team resists the pull toward feature parity and AI vocabulary.
