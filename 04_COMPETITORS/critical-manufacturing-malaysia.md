## IDENTITY

Company: Critical Manufacturing Malaysia Sdn Bhd
Parent: Critical Manufacturing (Portugal HQ), a subsidiary of ASMPT Limited (a listed Hong Kong-headquartered semiconductor/electronics equipment group)
Established in Malaysia: 2022
Location: George Town, Penang — SAME CITY as Mysoft MES's home base

## VERIFICATION

Confirmed via multiple independent sources: an official Critical Manufacturing press release, PR Newswire APAC, Evertiq, SMT Today, automation.com, and an independent third-party directory listing (Malaysian-German Chamber of Commerce member directory). Multiple independent outlets reporting the same announcement with consistent, specific detail (named local co-managing directors, incorporation year, address in George Town) is stronger evidence than a single press release alone.

Critical Manufacturing is also independently described (per the company and reprinted by outlets) as "a leader in the Gartner Magic Quadrant" for MES — this is a competitor claim citing a third-party analyst source; the underlying Gartner report itself was not directly reviewed in this research pass, so treat the "Magic Quadrant leader" claim as MEDIUM confidence pending direct verification.

## POSITIONING / BUSINESS MODEL

This is an enterprise-tier, high-tech-focused MES vendor — explicitly targeting semiconductor, electronics and other complex/high-mix manufacturing, which overlaps directly with several of Mysoft MES's named target industries (semiconductor, electronics manufacturing, precision engineering). Unlike most other Malaysian competitors found (which are SME-oriented systems integrators bundling MES with other services), Critical Manufacturing is a global enterprise software company with a dedicated, sophisticated MES product and a Gartner-recognized track record.

## GEOGRAPHIC RELEVANCE — HIGHEST OF ANY COMPETITOR FOUND

Critical Manufacturing Malaysia is physically based in Penang, the same city as Mysoft MES, and was set up specifically to serve "Southeast Asia's thriving high-tech manufacturing landscape." This is the most geographically and industry-relevant competitor identified in this research pass.

## COMPETITIVE IMPLICATIONS FOR MYSOFT MES

- Critical Manufacturing likely competes for larger, higher-complexity accounts (multi-site, semiconductor-grade traceability/compliance needs) rather than the SME/mid-market segment most other local competitors serve. This suggests a possible market segmentation: Mysoft may be better positioned against Critical Manufacturing on cost, implementation speed, and local relationship/support than on enterprise feature depth. This is a HYPOTHESIS requiring sales-team validation, not yet a confirmed pattern from won/lost deals.
- Being backed by ASMPT (a major semiconductor equipment maker) gives Critical Manufacturing credibility and account access into large local semiconductor players that a smaller local vendor may struggle to match.
