# DigiwinSoft Malaysia (Digiwin Software)

Confidence: VERIFIED (HIGH)
Research Date: 2026-09-01

## IDENTITY

Company: DigiwinSoft Malaysia (local arm of Digiwin Software, a Taiwan-origin enterprise software group)
Products: T100 ERP, ERP iGP, Smart Manufacturing Execution System (sMES), Shop Floor Tracking System (SFT), Warehouse Management System (WMS)
Website: digiwin.com.my

## VERIFICATION

Digiwin's own site documents multiple named, real Malaysian manufacturing customers with specific implementation outcomes (Tier 3 source, but corroborated by specificity and named companies rather than generic claims):
- EMICO — MES linked to machine scheduling and processing-program automation, standard-time-based lean production
- Wieson Automotive — MES for cost/waste reduction, per-material serial tracking, code-scan verification of product transfer
- FONTEC, Giving Industrial, Wegmans Furniture (Golden Eagle Award 2016 winner), SYK, Joly Beauty Packaging, Mankason Industry — additional named case studies across metal, plastics, furniture, automotive-parts manufacturing

The presence of multiple named customers with specific quantified or descriptive outcomes (not just generic marketing copy) is stronger evidence than a typical vendor "About Us" page — though these remain COMPETITOR CLAIMS (vendor-published case studies), not independently audited results.

## POSITIONING / BUSINESS MODEL

Digiwin is fundamentally an ERP vendor (T100 ERP) that layers MES/sMES on top as an execution-layer module, rather than a standalone MES specialist. This ERP-first architecture is a meaningful positioning difference from a dedicated shop-floor MES vendor: customers already on Digiwin ERP have a natural upsell path to sMES, which may be a structural advantage or lock-in risk depending on the account.

Industry focus: explicitly named as metal, plastic, furniture, and automotive parts manufacturing.

## GEOGRAPHIC / MARKET SCALE

Digiwin is a substantially larger, better-resourced organization than most other Malaysian MES vendors found in this research (backed by a Taiwan-headquartered software group with broader Southeast Asia presence). This makes it likely the best-resourced direct/indirect competitor identified, even though it is not Penang-specific.

## COMPETITIVE IMPLICATIONS FOR MYSOFT MES

- Digiwin's ERP+MES bundle competes most directly with manufacturers evaluating "one throat to choke" (single vendor for planning + execution) rather than a best-of-breed shop-floor MES like Mysoft. Positioning content that speaks to "why a dedicated shop-floor system may see things an ERP-bundled module misses" could be a differentiation angle — currently a HYPOTHESIS, not yet evidenced by an actual lost/won deal.
- Their case-study library (approach, format, named customers) is a useful creative benchmark to study further.
