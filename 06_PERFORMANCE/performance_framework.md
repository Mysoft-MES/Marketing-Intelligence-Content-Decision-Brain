# Performance Framework

## Awareness

Reach, views, 3-second retention, watch time, completion rate.

## Engagement

Comments, shares, saves, and engagement rate.

## Traffic

Click-through rate and landing-page visits.

## Lead Generation

Leads, conversion rate, qualified leads, and cost per lead.

## Revenue

Demo bookings, qualified opportunities, sales, revenue, and ROAS.

## Comparison Rules

- Select metrics based on the stated objective.
- Do not judge content using views alone.
- Compare like-for-like conditions where possible.
- Separate correlation from causation.
- Record uncontrolled variables and missing data.
